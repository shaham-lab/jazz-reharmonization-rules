Same-function recoloring

Functional substitution

tune: How Deep Is The Ocean ↑ rules
Swap a chord for another with the same harmonic function (Tonic / Subdominant / Dominant) in the key. Major keys demand each degree's diatonic seventh quality; minor keys admit qualities from the natural, harmonic, and melodic minors. The blues tonic dominant (I7) is never treated as a swappable tonic.
